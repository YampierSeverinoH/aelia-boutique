## Brand & Style

The design system is crafted for a high-end boutique experience, balancing modern minimalism with feminine grace. The brand personality is sophisticated, editorial, and curated. It targets a discerning audience that appreciates luxury, fashion-forward trends, and a seamless digital shopping environment.

The visual style is **Minimalism with Luxe Accents**. It relies on generous whitespace, precise typography, and a "light-as-air" interface that ensures product photography remains the focal point. While the interface is primarily white to maintain a clean aesthetic, delicate gold and rose-pink accents are used strategically to evoke a sense of premium craftsmanship and elegance.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in a neutral, high-key foundation of pure white and soft grays to maximize the impact of product imagery. The primary color is a dusty rose-pink (`#E5A8B1`), used for call-to-action highlights and interactive elements. The secondary color is a refined metallic gold (`#C5A059`), reserved for subtle decorative elements, iconography, and luxury markers.

Neutral tones move away from pure black to a deep "Ink Black" (`#1A1A1A`) for better readability and a softer high-fashion feel. Backgrounds should utilize "Blush Silk" (`#FDF3F4`) as a secondary surface color to provide gentle depth without competing with the primary white workspace.

## Typography

This design system employs a high-contrast typographic pairing to establish its luxury credentials. **Libre Caslon Text** is used for headlines and display elements, bringing a classic editorial authority and timeless elegance.

For functional text, **Hanken Grotesk** provides a sharp, contemporary contrast. It is highly legible at small sizes and features a technical precision that balances the romanticism of the serif typeface. Labels and navigation items should frequently use all-caps with generous letter-spacing to reinforce the boutique aesthetic.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout follows a **Fixed Grid** approach for desktop to maintain the editorial integrity of the site, with a maximum width of 1440px. A 12-column system is used for product grids and content layouts, allowing for flexible arrangements (e.g., asymmetrical product showcases).

Whitespace is treated as a core design element. Large vertical gaps (120px+) between homepage sections create a rhythmic, unhurried browsing experience. On mobile, margins are tighter (20px), but internal padding within cards and containers remains generous to prevent a cluttered appearance. Use a 4px baseline grid for small-scale alignment to ensure mathematical precision in the minimalist layout.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects heavy shadows in favor of **Tonal Layers** and **Low-contrast Outlines**. Depth is communicated through subtle shifts in background color (White vs. Blush Silk) and hair-line borders (`1px` width at 10-15% opacity).

Interactive states, such as hovering over a product image, should utilize "soft-focus" depth—subtle expansions of the element or extremely diffused, low-opacity ambient shadows that feel like a gentle lift rather than a harsh drop. The goal is to make the UI feel as light and tactile as the fabrics of the boutique.

## Shapes

The shape language is primarily **Soft (Level 1)**. While the overall layout feels structured and geometric, slightly softened corners (4px to 8px) on buttons and input fields prevent the design from feeling overly sharp or institutional.

Product cards and immersive image containers should maintain very tight corners to preserve the "high-fashion magazine" feel. Circular shapes are reserved exclusively for icon buttons (like "Add to Wishlist" or "Close") and decorative accents, referencing the circular motif in the brand logo.

## Components

### Buttons
Primary buttons are solid Ink Black with white text, featuring a subtle hover transition to Primary Pink. Secondary buttons use a transparent background with a 1px Gold border. All buttons use the `label-md` typographic style for a clean, uppercase appearance.

### Product Cards
Cards are borderless by default. On hover, the image should subtly scale or transition to an alternate view (e.g., detail shot or lifestyle view), and a "Quick Add" overlay should appear with a soft fade.

### Inputs & Fields
Input fields use a minimalist "bottom-border only" style for contact forms, while search and filter inputs use a fully enclosed box with a very light `soft-gray` background.

### Dynamic Cart
The shopping cart should be an "Off-canvas" drawer that slides from the right. It uses a high-contrast layout with clear typography and large product thumbnails to maintain the premium experience even during checkout.

### Chips & Tags
Used for "New In," "Sale," or "Limited Edition" markers. These should be small, rectangular, and use the `label-sm` font. Use gold backgrounds for "Exclusive" items and pink for "Sale" to maintain color semiotics.
